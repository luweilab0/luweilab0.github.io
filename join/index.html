The Laboratory of Molecular Cognition (Wei Lu Lab) at the Shenzhen Medical Academy of Research and Translation welcomes applicants interested in neuroscience research.

Open positions

  • Associate Researcher
  • Assistant Researcher
  • Postdoctoral Fellow
  • Lab Manager
  • Research Assistant

Research environment

Based at Weiguang Life Science Park in Shenzhen’s Guangming District, the laboratory studies the molecular and circuit mechanisms of synaptic function, anxiety and depression, and hypnosis and anesthesia. Projects emerge from broad, in-depth discussion of important scientific questions. We value an open, collaborative environment and systematic research training.

Our interdisciplinary research uses rodent models and combines molecular biology and biochemistry, patch-clamp electrophysiology, molecular genetics, EEG/EMG monitoring, in vivo calcium imaging, neural tracing, optogenetics and chemogenetics.

Application materials

  1. A complete CV in Chinese or English, including education, research experience and achievements, technical skills, awards and research interests.
  2. Applicants for Associate Researcher, Assistant Researcher and Postdoctoral Fellow positions should provide the details of two referees and ask them to send recommendation letters directly to gaba@smart.org.cn.
  3. Other materials demonstrating your research and professional capabilities.

How to apply

Please send your application materials as PDF attachments to gaba@smart.org.cn, using the subject line “Position–Name–Discipline”. Interviews will be arranged following review.

Additional information

Application materials will be kept confidential. Compensation will be discussed individually. Applications remain open until positions are filled.
