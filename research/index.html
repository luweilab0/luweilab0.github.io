Research

The Wei Lu laboratory studies synaptic development and the regulation of synaptic function, together with the neurobiological mechanisms underlying anxiety, depression, anesthesia and the effects of alcohol.

We use GABAergic synapses in rodents as a primary model system to investigate the molecular and neural circuit mechanisms of brain function and brain disorders.

By identifying and studying proteins that regulate synaptic function, we aim to understand synaptic mechanisms in health and disease and explore new therapeutic strategies targeting GABAᴀ receptors.

These studies aim to deepen our understanding of GABAergic synapses and their regulation in the brain, and identify potential drug targets for disorders including epilepsy, anxiety, insomnia and alcohol addiction.

Research methods

Our interdisciplinary research uses rodent models and combines molecular biology and biochemistry, patch-clamp electrophysiology, molecular genetics, EEG/EMG monitoring, in vivo calcium imaging, neural tracing, optogenetics and chemogenetics.
