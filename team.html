陆伟Wei Lu

资深研究员 Senior Investigator

陆伟博士于纽约大学获得博士学位（导师：Edward Ziff），随后在加州大学旧金山分校Roger Nicoll实验室从事博士后研究。2012–2025年任美国国立卫生研究院（NIH/NINDS）终身资深研究员，2025年全职加盟深圳医学科学院。

Dr. Wei Lu received his PhD at New York University (advisor: Edward Ziff) and completed postdoctoral training in the lab of Roger Nicoll at UCSF. He was a tenured Senior Investigator at NIH/NINDS from 2012 to 2025, and joined SMART full-time in 2025.

荣誉：2019年NIH NINDS所长奖；2011年NIH NIMH首席研究员发展奖。

Honors: 2019 NIH NINDS Director's Award; 2011 NIH NIMH Principal Investigator Development Award.
