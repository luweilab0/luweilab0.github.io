Lab Philosophy

We believe in free, open, and collaborative research, where projects arise from broad and deep discussion. We study GABAergic synapses using techniques ranging from molecular biology to in vivo imaging and optogenetics.

Open Positions

The lab recruits in three categories: postdoctoral fellows, who lead independent projects on GABAergic synapses (applicants with backgrounds in neuroscience, molecular biology, or related fields are welcome); PhD students, admitted through the SMART PhD program and trained in the lab; and research assistants, who take part in the lab’s experiments and support ongoing projects.

How to Apply

Please send your CV to gaba@smart.org.cn.

For more information, see the SMART career page.
