Team

Dr. Wei Lu (SMART faculty page)

Senior Investigator

luwei@smart.org.cn

Wei Lu received his PhD at New York University (advisor: Edward Ziff) and completed postdoctoral training in the lab of Roger Nicoll at UCSF. He was a tenured Senior Investigator at NIH/NINDS from 2012 to 2025, and joined SMART full-time in 2025.

Honors: 2019 NIH NINDS Director's Award; 2011 NIH NIMH Principal Investigator Development Award.
