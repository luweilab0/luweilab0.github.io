实验室理念Lab Philosophy

我们倡导自由、开放、协作的研究文化——课题源于广泛而深入的讨论。实验室围绕GABA能突触开展研究，运用从分子生物学到在体成像与光遗传学的多种技术。 We believe in free, open, and collaborative research, where projects arise from broad and deep discussion. We study GABAergic synapses using techniques ranging from molecular biology to in vivo imaging and optogenetics.

开放职位Open Positions

实验室常年招收三类成员：博士后——主导GABA能突触方向的独立研究课题，欢迎神经科学、分子生物学或相关领域背景的博士毕业生申请；博士研究生——通过深圳医学科学院（SMART）博士项目招收，在实验室接受系统的科研训练；研究助理——参与实验室的实验与日常科研工作，支持各研究课题的开展。 The lab recruits in three categories: postdoctoral fellows, who lead independent projects on GABAergic synapses (applicants with backgrounds in neuroscience, molecular biology, or related fields are welcome); PhD students, admitted through the SMART PhD program and trained in the lab; and research assistants, who take part in the lab's experiments and support ongoing projects.
