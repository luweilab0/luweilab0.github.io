Wei Lu

Senior Investigator · Principal Investigator

luwei@smart.org.cnSMART faculty profile ↗

Biography

Wei Lu received his PhD from New York University under the supervision of Edward Ziff, studying the biochemical properties of neuronal glutamate receptors and their interacting proteins.

He then conducted postdoctoral research in Roger Nicoll’s laboratory at the University of California, San Francisco, combining electrophysiology with single-cell genetics to investigate the molecular mechanisms of excitatory synaptic transmission.

From 2012 to 2025, Wei Lu worked at the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke, National Institutes of Health (NIH/NINDS), serving as an Investigator and subsequently a Senior Investigator. He joined the Shenzhen Medical Academy of Research and Translation full time in 2025.

His research focuses on synaptic development and function in health and disease, and the synaptic mechanisms of neurological disorders. He has published more than 50 papers in journals including Science, Cell, Neuron, PNAS and Nature Communications.

Wei Lu aims to build a free, open and collaborative research environment, providing systematic scientific training and opportunities for professional development.
